# MOLAR — Master Website & Funnel Strategy

## Vision

Merge the existing MOLAR website and the $97 Patient Education Library funnel into **one premium master website**.

The goal is to position MOLAR not simply as a social media agency or video library, but as:

> **The content infrastructure for modern dental practices.**

The website should feel like a **premium SaaS + healthcare technology platform** rather than a traditional marketing agency.

---

# 1. Core Positioning

## Current perception

Visitors may currently see MOLAR as:

> “A dental social media service that also has a patient education video library.”

## New perception

Position MOLAR as:

> **MOLAR is the content engine for modern dental practices.**

MOLAR provides one connected content ecosystem across:

- Social media
- Patient education
- Chairside treatment explanations
- Consultation rooms
- Waiting-room TV loops
- Practice resources
- Multi-language/global content

Social media, Patient Education, and MOLAR TV become individual products inside one larger ecosystem.

# 18. Design Direction

The website should feel like:

**Premium SaaS × Healthcare Technology × High-End Creative Agency**

Avoid making it look like:

- A generic AI startup
- A cheap social media agency
- A template marketplace
- A basic dental website
- A generic WordPress marketing site

## Visual principles

### Typography

Use a strong modern sans-serif.

Prioritize:

- Large confident headlines
- Tight hierarchy
- High readability
- Generous whitespace

### Color

Use MOLAR's existing brand identity as the foundation.

Keep the palette restrained.

Avoid excessive gradients.

### UI

Use:

- Glass/light surfaces where appropriate
- Clean cards
- Real product screenshots
- Video previews
- Subtle borders
- Strong spacing
- Premium motion

### Imagery

Prioritize:

1. Real client content
2. Real dentists/practices
3. Real product UI
4. Real patient education videos
5. High-quality dental environments

Avoid generic AI-generated dentist stock imagery wherever possible.
